# Resources page verification (2026 figures unofficial; certification is scheduled for Dec. 1, 2026)

Scope: every number and every equipment and addressee cell on the wiki **Resources** page, the 105 county clerk letters, the Attorney General letter, the clerk-letter ZIP packages and `KS_105_County_Voting_Equipment_VerifiedVoting.csv`.

## Two independent passes
- **Pass 1 (builders):** `scripts/resources/build_equipment.py`, `build_clerk_letters.py`, `build_charts.py` and `build_resources_page.py`. These use pandas and the pass A county table (`scripts/pass_a.py`).
- **Pass 2 (checker):** `scripts/resources/verify_resources.py`. It uses no pandas and no pass A output. It works from the pass B county table (`scripts/pass_b.py`: openpyxl, csv, regex and exact fractions on the raw SOS files), the raw Verified Voting CSVs and county API files (csv and json modules), the raw SOS county election officer list (regex), the Census 2025 county estimates, the KDOT district list and the ZIP files on disk. All arithmetic uses exact fractions.

Pass 2 checks:
1. The 105-row equipment table, cell by cell (FIPS, population, marking method, vendor, election-day, mail and accessible equipment, VVPAT, versions, status, source link, change, central-count and hybrid notes), plus the sort order.
2. The vendor summary counts, populations and percentages, the marking-method counts, and the central-count, changed-equipment, renamed-vendor, paper-poll-book and hardware-version lists.
3. The equipment CSV against the raw files.
4. ZIP contents (105 PDFs and 105 Word files; each district's count), ZIP sizes as printed, the district table, and full coverage of the 105 counties.
5. All 105 clerk letter PDFs (text extracted with pdftotext). Checked: addressee name, title, address and ZIP against the SOS list; the salutation; the three county figure lines; the statewide sentence; the pattern sentence; the election-day equipment; and that the Attorney General race is absent.
6. Chart captions: counties where the Democratic share rose or fell, and the five ceiling ΔG values, against the chart builder's JSON.
7. The surplus fits (slope, intercept, R²), recomputed by exact OLS.
8. The ten-county flip table: every cell, recomputed as D26 − D22 × (R26/R22).
9. A whole-page scan. Every remaining numeric token on the page must equal an independently recomputed value or a listed structural constant (years, statute section numbers, Federal Register citation, county counts, dates).

**Result (Nov 24, 2026): 2,984 checks, 0 mismatches, 0 unexplained numeric tokens** (`verify_resources_pass2.json`).

The county figures behind the page and letters come from `pass_a.py` and `pass_b.py`, re-run Nov 24, 2026. Statewide totals agree exactly in both passes: Democratic registration 523,317 → 521,669; Democratic votes for governor 499,849 → 551,243*; total votes for governor 1,008,998 → 1,094,842*.

## Quotations
`verify_quotes.py` checks every quotation of eight or more characters in the AG letter (26 quotes) and in each clerk letter (2 quotes each) against the saved source texts: statutes from kslegislature.gov, AG Opinion 2025-25, the 2023 Legislative Post Audit report and Executive Order 14399. **0 not found** (`quote_check.json`).

## Why the older wiki checker does not cover this page
`scripts/verify_wiki.py` checks the analysis pages generated by `build_wiki.py`. It does not read Resources.md, the letters, the ZIPs or the Verified Voting equipment data, so this separate checker was added.

## Known gaps
- The SOS county voting-system list (sos.ks.gov/forms/elections/voting%20systems.pdf) currently returns "Page Not Found", and the archived copy could not be downloaded. Equipment therefore comes from Verified Voting ("The Verifier – November 2026", data as of Nov 24, 2026), the same source the Texas page uses.
- Verified Voting lists no firmware or software versions for any Kansas county. A hardware version ("HW 3.0") appears only for the ExpressVote in five counties.
- Clerk names and addresses come from the SOS county election officer list retrieved Nov 24, 2026. Each letter asks the office to confirm the addressee.
- All letters are unsent drafts.
